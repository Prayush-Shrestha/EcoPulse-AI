- The validation-selected model also had the lowest test RMSE, which supports the selection.

10. LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The experiment forecasts one hour ahead from observed history; recursive
  multi-day forecasts were not evaluated.
- The household data contain no weather, occupancy, holiday or tariff
  variables, so exogenous drivers are absent.
- Only one household (France, 2006-2010) is modelled; results do not
  automatically generalise to other homes or regions.
- Feature warm-up and incomplete hours remove 3,138 hourly rows; the remaining rows are not a random sample of the original period.
- A single chronological hold-out is used, not rolling-origin backtesting.
- Hyperparameters were fixed a priori; no exhaustive tuning was performed.
- Random Forest and HistGradientBoosting cannot extrapolate to consumption
  levels outside their training range.
- MAPE divides by actual consumption and becomes unstable for very small
  hourly values even when it is mathematically defined.
